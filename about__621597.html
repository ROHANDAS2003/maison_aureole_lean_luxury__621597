About the maison

Luxury with restraint.

Maison Auréole is imagined as a modern fragrance house where silence is a material, negative space is a signature, and gold is used only when it earns attention.

Brand belief

Presence should not have to announce itself.

Our identity is built on composed contrast: white space for breath, black for structure and gold for warmth. The result is a premium world that feels intimate, tactile and collectible.

House principles

The Auréole method.

Each object is designed as part of a complete ritual rather than a standalone purchase.

01

Compose around atmosphere first: the room, the skin, the hour and the silence around the wearer.

02

Edit until only the essential notes remain — no clutter, no excess sweetness, no decorative noise.

03

Finish every object with a tactile detail: rounded glass, ceramic weight, soft oil or a gold-lit label.
